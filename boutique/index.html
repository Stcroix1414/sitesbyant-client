The shop

Everything in here was picked up, turned over, and loved a little before it went on the shelf.

Golden Hour started as a garage-sale habit that outgrew the garage. Now it's four hundred square feet on Campbell Ave where the inventory changes weekly and nothing is sorted quite alphabetically.

Half the shop is vintage — glassware, textiles, cameras, the occasional chair that's hard to say goodbye to. The other half is new work from local makers: candles, cards, ceramics, small-batch everything.

Come in to find a gift; stay because the light in here at 5pm is exactly what the name promises.
